THE GUARDIAN'S REALM

Overview The Lore Gameplay

The Descent into the Iron Crypt

For centuries, the Iron Crypt has served as a graveyard for would-be heroes. You begin your journey in the upper sanctums, armed with a relic that channels your very essence into bolts of light. The corridors are narrow, choked with the skeletal remains of those who failed, and swarming with lesser demons—scouts for the darkness that lies beneath.

The Hermit of the Depths: Jonesy

Deep within the darkness, where the air grows cold and the stone turns black, you encounter Jonesy. He is a legend among the survivors—a warrior who entered the Crypt decades ago and never found the exit.

Jonesy provides more than just a brief respite; he offers the hard-earned wisdom of a man who has seen the Miniboss’s blade firsthand. "The shadows move here," he whispers, "but they are nothing compared to the Eye in the Void." His presence is the only thing keeping your sanity intact before the final push.

The Great Ascension

At the heart of the Crypt stands the Gatekeeper, a hulking Miniboss tasked with ensuring no one ever leaves. Upon its defeat, the ancient magic holding the Crypt together unravels.

Instead of the surface, you are pulled upward into the Astral Rift—a platform floating in a starless sky. Here, the true master of the Crypt reveals itself: The Guardian. An eternal sentinel designed to test your reflexes and resolve in a storm of magic and steel. Victory means freedom; failure means becoming another ghost for Jonesy to talk to.
