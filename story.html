THE GUARDIAN'S REALM

Overview The Lore Gameplay

The Descent

You wake up in the Dungeon Depths. Armed with nothing but a basic projectile, you must navigate through narrow corridors filled with small enemies that grow stronger as you descend.

An Unlikely Ally

Deep within the ruins, you encounter Jonesy. He is the only friendly face in this hellscape, offering weapon modifications and armor upgrades (Blue Armor) in exchange for the XP you've gathered from fallen foes.

The Rift

After defeating the Dungeon Miniboss, a violent magical surge teleports you away from the damp stone walls and into a celestial void. Waiting there is The Guardian—your final and most lethal test.
