THE GUARDIAN'S REALM

A GameMaker Studio Action-RPG

The Lore

1. The Forgotten Dungeon

Your journey begins in the depths of a crumbling, forgotten dungeon. Stripped of your past and armed with only basic gear, you must fight your way through hordes of small, relentless enemies lurking in the shadows. Every enemy defeated earns you valuable experience to grow your stamina and strength.

2. An Unlikely Ally: Jonesy

Amidst the chaos, you discover safe havens within the labyrinth. Here, you encounter a mysterious merchant named Jonesy. Offering a moment of respite, Jonesy trades fragments you've collected for powerful armor (like the Blue Armor for added defense) and weapon upgrades (such as the Laser Projectile) vital for your survival.

3. The Miniboss & The Rift

At the dungeon's exit stands a brutal Miniboss. It acts as the gatekeeper to freedom. But victory is an illusion—landing the final blow triggers a volatile magical rift, violently teleporting you out of the dungeon and into a celestial void.

4. The Guardian

You awaken in an inescapable arena facing the ultimate trial: The Guardian. A relentless entity utilizing bullet-hell attacks, spiraling projectiles, and lightning-fast tracking dashes. Only by mastering your newfound upgrades from Jonesy can you hope to survive the Guardian's onslaught.
