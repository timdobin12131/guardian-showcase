Gameplay Showcases

Below are highlights from the game, including mechanics, the upgrade system, and boss encounters.

1. Dungeon Crawling

Exploring the initial dungeon, utilizing the 360-degree cursor aiming, and fighting off small enemy waves.

2. Meeting Jonesy (Upgrades)

Interacting with Jonesy in the safe room to purchase armor upgrades and new projectile types.

3. The Miniboss Fight & Teleportation

Defeating the dungeon gatekeeper and getting sucked into the boss rift.

4. The Guardian: Bullet Hell Phase

Dodging The Guardian's deadly spiral and spread-shot patterns using stamina-dashing.

5. The Guardian: Dash & Wall Burst

The Guardian locks onto the player's position and dashes. Missing the player triggers a massive 360-degree projectile burst upon hitting the wall.
