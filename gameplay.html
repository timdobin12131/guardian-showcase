Project Description The Story Gameplay Showcase

Boss Battle: The Guardian

After fighting through the dungeon and surviving the rift, the player faces the ultimate challenge. This video showcases the state-machine AI, bullet-hell patterns, and the reactive dash-and-burst mechanic.

Mechanics Displayed:

  • Spiral Attack: Precise projectile spacing for tight dodging.
  • Telegraphed Dash: A red flash warning before a high-speed charge.
  • Wall Burst: A 360-degree projectile fallback if the boss misses the player.
  • Dynamic UI: Real-time Boss HP bar and Player stamina management.
