THE GUARDIAN'S REALM

Overview The Lore Gameplay

Survive the Crypt. Break the Code.

The Guardian's Realm is a fast-paced, top-down Action-RPG built from the ground up in GameMaker Studio. It combines the claustrophobic tension of dungeon crawling with the high-octane reflexes required of a bullet hell shooter.

Core Mechanics

  • 360-Degree Combat: Fluid mouse-aiming mechanics that allow for precision shooting while retreating or dodging.
  • Dynamic Encounters: Face increasingly difficult waves of enemies before clashing with the Gatekeeper Miniboss.
  • Progression System: Gather resources from fallen foes to trade with NPCs for crucial armor and weapon modifications.

The Final Test

The game culminates in a massive, multi-phase encounter against The Guardian. This boss utilizes a custom State-Machine AI, seamlessly transitioning between tight spiral-projectile attacks, telegraphed high-speed dashes, and punishing 360-degree wall bursts.

Development Details

Engine: GameMaker Studio 2

Language: GameMaker Language (GML)

Highlights: Advanced collision detection, alarm-based invincibility frames, and procedural projectile math.
